The Coach's Automation Map

A one-week task audit that shows you exactly what to keep, automate, delegate or drop.

By Coach David Adeoye Abodunrin
AI & Automation Coach · Master Futurist · Founder, DeoCoach

For one week, write down every task you do for your practice. At the end of the week, label each one and choose a tool. Print this page twice if you need more rows.

Labels: K = Keep (only you, and clients feel it) · A = Automate (repeats, no judgement) · D = Delegate (needs a person, not you) · X = Drop (no one would miss it)
TaskAreaTimes a weekMinutes eachK / A / D / XTool or person

Score your map

  1. Add up the minutes for every task marked A and D. That is the time you can win back each week: ______ minutes.
  2. Circle the three A tasks that cost you most. These are your first automations.
  3. Pick one D task to hand over this month. Who could do it? ____________________
  4. Cross out every X task today.

Areas to check

Client delivery · Admin · Marketing · Sales · Finance · Growth. If an area has no tasks written down, you may be avoiding it — that is useful to know too.
