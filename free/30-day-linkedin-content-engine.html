The 30-Day LinkedIn Content Engine for Coaches

Thirty post ideas, a simple structure for every post, and a prompt that turns each idea into a finished draft.

By Coach David Adeoye Abodunrin
AI & Automation Coach · Master Futurist · Founder, DeoCoach

The post structure that works

  1. Hook — one line that stops the scroll: a question, a surprising truth or a short story opening.
  2. Body — three to five short paragraphs. One idea only.
  3. Lesson — what the reader can take away today.
  4. Invitation — a question for the comments, or a gentle call to action.
Rhythm: post five days a week for six weeks. Reply to every comment within the day. Twice a week, comment thoughtfully on posts by people your buyers follow.

The prompt that drafts each post

I'm a [niche] coach in [city]. Write a LinkedIn post on this idea: [idea from the list]. Use this structure: a one-line hook, three short paragraphs, one practical lesson, and a question to invite comments. 120–180 words. Warm, direct, no hype, no emojis except one at most. End with three relevant hashtags.

Always add one real detail from your own experience before posting. That is what makes it yours.
