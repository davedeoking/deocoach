The AI & Automation Starter Kit for Coaches

A 90-day method to map your practice, automate the right work, govern it safely and turn the hours you win back into income.

By Coach David Adeoye Abodunrin
AI & Automation Coach · Master Futurist · Founder, DeoCoach
The promise. In 90 days you go from curious about AI to running with it: your work mapped, five automations live, your clients' data protected, and new income switched on. You do not need to be technical. You need one hour a week and the courage to start small.

Why now

AI will not replace the coach. It will replace the coach's admin, research and guesswork, and it will reward the coaches who use it. Clients now expect evidence, speed and follow-through. The coach who answers within the hour, arrives at every session prepared and shows progress in writing wins the work.

The method below has four moves. Do them in order. Each move has one output you can hold in your hand.

Move 1 — Map (weeks 1–2)

For one week, write down everything you do for your practice, in 15-minute blocks or more. Use the Automation Map worksheet in this toolkit. Group tasks into six areas:

  1. Client delivery — sessions, preparation, notes, homework, progress reports.
  2. Admin — scheduling, reminders, rescheduling, filing, emails.
  3. Marketing — posts, newsletters, talks, testimonials.
  4. Sales — enquiries, discovery calls, proposals, follow-ups.
  5. Finance — invoices, receipts, chasing payments.
  6. Growth — learning, supervision, partnerships.

Then give every task one label:

  • Keep — only you can do it, and it is where clients feel your value (the session itself, hard conversations, judgement).
  • Automate — it repeats, follows a pattern and needs no judgement in the moment.
  • Delegate — it needs a human, but not you (a virtual assistant, a junior coach, a family member who helps with admin).
  • Drop — nobody would miss it.
The five-minute rule. Any task you do more than twice a week that takes more than five minutes is a candidate for automation.

Move 2 — Automate (weeks 3–6): your first five automations

1. Booking without the back-and-forth

Replace "When are you free?" emails with one booking link that shows your available times, collects the client's details and sends reminders automatically. Free options exist in Google Calendar (appointment schedules), Microsoft Bookings and Calendly's free plan.

  • Set reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before every session.
  • Add two questions to the booking form: "What would make this session valuable?" and "Anything I should know beforehand?"
  • Put the link in your email signature, WhatsApp Business profile and LinkedIn.

2. Session notes in minutes, not an hour

After each session, spend three minutes speaking or typing rough bullet points. Then ask an AI assistant to turn them into a clean summary using the prompt below. If you record sessions with a transcription tool, always ask permission first and tell the client where the recording is kept and when it is deleted.

You are my coaching assistant. Turn these rough notes into a session summary with four headings: What we explored, Insights the client named, Agreed actions (with dates), Questions to hold until next time. Use the client's own words where I quoted them. Do not add anything I did not write. Notes: [paste notes, with names removed]

3. Follow-up within the hour

Speed is a promise. Use the summary to send a short follow-up within an hour of each session. Save it as a template so it takes two minutes.

Write a warm, three-paragraph follow-up email to my coaching client. Paragraph 1: thank them and name one moment of courage from today. Paragraph 2: list the actions they agreed, with dates. Paragraph 3: remind them of our next session and how to reach me. Under 150 words. British English. Summary: [paste summary]

4. One idea, five pieces of content

Every week, pick one idea from your sessions (never a client's story without permission) and let AI help you shape it into a LinkedIn post, a WhatsApp status, a short email to your list, three quotes for images, and a talk outline. Use the 30-Day LinkedIn Content Engine in this toolkit for ideas.

Here is one idea from my coaching work: [idea]. Write: (1) a LinkedIn post of 120–180 words that opens with a question; (2) a 40-word WhatsApp status; (3) three short quotable lines; (4) a five-point outline for a 10-minute talk. Keep my voice: warm, direct, practical. No hashtags except three at the end of the LinkedIn post.

5. Proposals and invoices from templates

After a discovery call, paste your notes into an AI assistant to draft the proposal, then check every word. Keep one proposal template and one invoice template. Use an invoicing tool or payment links (for example Paystack or Flutterwave in Africa, Stripe or PayPal elsewhere) so clients can pay in one click.

Draft a one-page coaching proposal from these discovery-call notes. Sections: Where you are now, Where you want to be, How we will work (sessions, format, dates), Investment (three options: Essential, Growth, Premium), Next step. Use the client's words for their goals. Notes: [paste notes, no confidential details]

Move 3 — Govern (weeks 7–8)

Trust is the coach's only real currency. Before any client information goes near an AI tool, ask three questions:

  1. Where does my client's data go? Which company, which country, and is it used to train their models?
  2. Who can see it? Only you, or the tool's staff and partners too?
  3. Can I delete it? Easily, and completely?

If you cannot answer all three, do not use that tool with client information. Use the Client Data & AI Safety Checklist in this toolkit to write your one-page AI policy.

Never automate: judgement about a person's wellbeing, anything to do with safeguarding or crisis, the decision to take on or end a client, or the words of a difficult conversation.

Move 4 — Monetise (weeks 9–12)

Most coaches who automate win back four to eight hours a week. Decide now what those hours are for, or they will disappear into busyness. Choose one:

  • A new package — use the 3-Tier Package Builder to design Essential, Growth and Premium offers.
  • A group programme — turn your best one-to-one journey into a six-week cohort for eight to twelve people.
  • A new market — pick one city and one sector you have never served. Use DeoCoach's Horizon Atlas and City Minds to learn what they care about.
  • Visibility — join one professional body where your buyers gather and offer to speak.
