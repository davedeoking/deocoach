The 3-Tier Package Builder

Stop selling hours. Design Essential, Growth and Premium packages your clients can say yes to.

By Coach David Adeoye Abodunrin
AI & Automation Coach · Master Futurist · Founder, DeoCoach

Why three tiers

When people see one price, they ask "yes or no?". When they see three, they ask "which one?". The middle option is usually chosen most, so design it as the one you most want to sell.

Step 1 — Name the outcome

Your packages sell a result, not sessions. Complete this sentence:

I help [who] to [achieve what] in [how long], without [what they fear].

Step 3 — Price with confidence

  • Essential should be easy to say yes to, but still profitable.
  • Growth is roughly twice Essential and includes the support that gets results.
  • Premium is three to five times Essential: more access, more depth, more speed.
  • Offer instalments for Growth and Premium.
  • In mixed markets, set a local-currency price and an international price.

Step 4 — Test it

Show your three tiers to five people who match your ideal client. Ask: "Which would you choose, and why?" Adjust names, contents and prices before you publish.
