Client Data & AI Safety Checklist

Fifteen checks, consent wording and a one-page AI policy template — so you can use AI without risking your clients' trust.

By Coach David Adeoye Abodunrin
AI & Automation Coach · Master Futurist · Founder, DeoCoach

Written by a coach who holds four ISACA credentials in security, audit, governance and privacy. This is practical guidance, not legal advice: check the data-protection law where you practise (for example the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, Kenya's Data Protection Act or South Africa's POPIA).

The three questions

  1. Where does my client's data go?
  2. Who can see it?
  3. Can I delete it?

If you cannot answer all three for a tool, do not use it with client information.

Fifteen checks

  • I know which AI tools I use with any client information, and I have listed them.
  • For each tool, I know where data is stored and whether it is used to train the provider's models.
  • I have switched off model training on my client-facing tools where the setting exists.
  • I never paste names, contact details, health information or identifiable stories into AI tools.
  • I ask clients for consent before recording or transcribing a session, and I tell them where recordings go.
  • Recordings and transcripts are deleted on a schedule I can state (for example, 30 days).
  • My devices are protected by a password or passcode and up to date.
  • I use two-step verification on email, cloud storage and payment accounts.
  • Client files are stored in one place, not scattered across WhatsApp, email and downloads.
  • I share documents by secure link with access control, not as open attachments where possible.
  • My contract or welcome pack explains how I handle data and AI, in plain words.
  • I know what I would do if a device were lost or an account hacked.
  • I never use AI to make judgements about a client's wellbeing, safety or mental health.
  • I review my tools and settings every six months.
  • I can delete a client's data completely if they ask.

Consent wording you can adapt

"With your permission, I'd like to record our session so I can focus on you rather than note-taking. The recording is transcribed by [tool], stored in [place] and deleted after [30] days. It is never shared and never used to train AI. You can say no, or ask me to stop recording at any time, and it won't affect our work together."

One-page AI policy template

SectionYour words
PurposeI use AI tools to save time on admin and preparation so I can give clients more attention. AI never replaces my judgement.
Tools I use[List each tool and what it is used for]
What never goes into AINames, contact details, health information, identifiable stories, anything a client asks me to keep private.
RecordingsOnly with consent; stored in [place]; deleted after [days].
Client rightsClients can ask what I hold, ask me to correct it or ask me to delete it.
ReviewI review this policy every six months. Last reviewed: [date].
