Beta development tool. DeoCoach is an unvalidated development and planning tool for coaches. It is not a psychological, clinical or selection instrument and must not be used to hire, screen or select people. Sonar, Niche Radar and City Minds are scenario-based foresight — DeoCoach planning estimates to explore and test, not guarantees or audited market data. Statistics in the Evidence Bank are cited to their sources; check the latest figures before quoting them. Competency areas are informed by, not copied from, recognised coaching frameworks.
